# Headache and stomach pain (sir dard, pet dard)
Keywords: headache, sir dard, सिर दर्द, stomach pain, pet dard, पेट दर्द, gas, acidity, vomiting, migraine

## Headache
Common headaches come from tiredness, poor sleep, dehydration, missed meals, eye strain or stress. Rest in a quiet place, drink water, eat on time.
See a doctor if headaches are frequent or getting worse. Check blood pressure if you have headache and are on BP medicine.
Go to hospital at once for a sudden, very severe headache, headache after a head injury, or headache with vomiting, blurred vision, weakness, confusion, trouble speaking or fits.
सिर दर्द में आराम करें, पानी पिएँ, समय पर खाना खाएँ। अचानक बहुत तेज़ दर्द, चोट के बाद दर्द, उल्टी, धुँधला दिखना या बोलने में दिक्कत हो तो तुरंत अस्पताल जाएँ।

## Stomach pain
Mild stomach pain can come from gas, acidity, indigestion or a stomach infection. Eat light food, avoid oily and spicy food, drink clean water.
Note where the pain is, when it started and what makes it worse; this helps the doctor.
See a doctor if pain is severe or increasing, lasts more than a day, comes with fever or repeated vomiting, or the abdomen is hard. Go at once for black or bloody stools, vomiting blood, or severe pain in the lower right abdomen.
पेट में तेज़ या बढ़ता दर्द, बुखार के साथ दर्द, लगातार उल्टी, काला या खूनी मल हो तो तुरंत डॉक्टर के पास जाएँ।
