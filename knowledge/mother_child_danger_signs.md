# Pregnancy and child danger signs
Keywords: pregnancy, pregnant, garbhwati, गर्भवती, delivery, newborn, baby, child, bachcha, बच्चा, ASHA, ANC, JSSK

## Pregnancy danger signs
A pregnant woman should go to hospital at once, or call 108, for: heavy vaginal bleeding, severe headache, blurred vision, swelling of face or hands, fits, severe abdominal pain, fever, water breaking before time, or baby moving much less.
Register the pregnancy with the ASHA worker or health centre, attend regular antenatal check-ups, take iron-folic acid tablets, and plan for delivery in a health facility. Government schemes such as Janani Shishu Suraksha Karyakram provide free delivery care and transport at public facilities; ask the ASHA worker.
गर्भवती महिला को तेज़ रक्तस्राव, तेज़ सिरदर्द, धुँधला दिखना, चेहरे/हाथों में सूजन, दौरे या बच्चे की हलचल कम होने पर तुरंत अस्पताल जाना चाहिए या 108 बुलाना चाहिए।

## Child danger signs
Take a child to a doctor at once, or call 108, if the child cannot drink or breastfeed, vomits everything, has fits, is very sleepy or hard to wake, has fast breathing or chest pulling in, or has high fever with stiff neck.
Keep up breastfeeding, complete the vaccination schedule on time through the anganwadi or health centre, and wash hands before feeding.
बच्चा दूध/पानी न पी पाए, सब कुछ उल्टी कर दे, दौरे आएँ, बहुत सुस्त हो या तेज़ साँस ले तो तुरंत डॉक्टर के पास जाएँ।
