# Cough, cold, breathing and TB (khansi, jukam)
Keywords: cough, cold, khansi, खांसी, jukam, जुकाम, sardi, TB, tuberculosis, breathlessness, asthma, pneumonia

## Common cold and cough
A common cold with runny nose, sore throat and mild cough usually settles in 5 to 7 days. Rest, drink warm fluids, take steam, and cover the mouth when coughing.
Avoid smoke from chulha, tobacco, and dust. Antibiotics do not work on colds; do not take them without a doctor's advice.
सर्दी-जुकाम आमतौर पर 5-7 दिन में ठीक हो जाता है। गुनगुना पानी पिएँ, भाप लें, आराम करें। बिना डॉक्टर की सलाह के एंटीबायोटिक न लें।

## Cough for more than 2 weeks: get tested for TB
A cough lasting more than 2 weeks, especially with fever, night sweats, weight loss or blood in sputum, needs a TB test. Sputum testing and TB treatment are free at government health facilities under the national TB programme.
Do not wait. Early treatment cures TB and stops it spreading to the family. Cover the mouth when coughing and keep the room ventilated.
2 हफ़्ते से ज़्यादा खांसी हो, बुखार, रात को पसीना, वज़न कम होना या बलगम में खून आए तो टीबी की जाँच कराएँ। सरकारी अस्पताल में जाँच और इलाज मुफ़्त है।

## Breathing difficulty: danger signs
Fast breathing, chest pulling in, wheezing, blue lips, or being unable to speak full sentences are danger signs. In a child, fast breathing with cough can be pneumonia and needs a doctor the same day.
Severe breathlessness is an emergency: call 108.
