# Blood pressure, diabetes and anaemia
Keywords: BP, blood pressure, high BP, hypertension, sugar, diabetes, madhumeh, anaemia, anemia, khoon ki kami, hemoglobin, haemoglobin, Hb, report

## Blood pressure (BP)
Normal blood pressure is below 120/80 mmHg. A reading of 140/90 mmHg or more on repeated days suggests high blood pressure; a doctor must confirm.
High BP often has no symptoms. It raises the risk of stroke, heart attack and kidney problems. Reduce salt (less than 5 grams a day, about one teaspoon), stay active, avoid tobacco and alcohol, and keep a healthy weight.
Never stop BP medicine without asking the doctor. Take it daily even if you feel fine.
Very high BP (180/120 or more) with headache, chest pain, breathlessness or weakness is an emergency: call 108.
BP 140/90 या उससे ऊपर लगातार आए तो डॉक्टर को दिखाएँ। नमक कम करें। BP की दवा बिना डॉक्टर से पूछे बंद न करें।

## Diabetes and blood sugar
Fasting blood sugar of 70 to 99 mg/dL is normal. 100 to 125 is raised (pre-diabetes). 126 or more on repeat testing suggests diabetes; only a doctor can confirm.
Symptoms: excess thirst, frequent urination, tiredness, weight loss, slow-healing wounds. Healthy diet, daily walking and less sugar help. Check feet daily for cuts and wear footwear.
Low sugar (shaking, sweating, dizziness) in a person on diabetes medicine needs quick sugar and medical help.
खाली पेट शुगर 70-99 सामान्य है, 100-125 थोड़ी ज़्यादा, और 126 या अधिक दोबारा जाँच में आए तो डॉक्टर डायबिटीज़ की पुष्टि करेंगे।

## Anaemia (khoon ki kami)
Haemoglobin below 12 g/dL in women and below 13 g/dL in men suggests anaemia. Symptoms: tiredness, pale skin, dizziness, breathlessness on effort.
Eat iron-rich food: green leafy vegetables, dal, jaggery, beans, and add vitamin C (lemon, amla) to help absorption. Government programmes give free iron-folic acid (IFA) tablets to children, adolescent girls and pregnant women; take them as advised.
Pregnant women with anaemia need regular antenatal check-ups. Very low haemoglobin needs a doctor soon.
हीमोग्लोबिन महिलाओं में 12 और पुरुषों में 13 से कम हो तो खून की कमी हो सकती है। हरी सब्ज़ी, दाल, गुड़ खाएँ; आयरन-फोलिक एसिड की गोली सरकारी केंद्र से मुफ़्त मिलती है।
