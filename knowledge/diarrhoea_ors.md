# Diarrhoea, vomiting and ORS (dast, ulti)
Keywords: diarrhoea, loose motion, dast, दस्त, ulti, उल्टी, vomiting, ORS, dehydration, paani ki kami

## Diarrhoea: prevent dehydration with ORS
The main danger of diarrhoea and vomiting is loss of body water (dehydration). Give ORS (oral rehydration solution) early.
Mix one full ORS packet in one litre of clean boiled and cooled water. Give small sips often, and after each loose stool. If the person vomits, wait 10 minutes and continue slowly.
Keep eating light food. Continue breastfeeding and normal feeding for children. Do not stop food or milk. Zinc tablets are recommended for children with diarrhoea; ask the health worker for the right dose.
दस्त या उल्टी में ORS का घोल (एक पैकेट एक लीटर साफ़ उबले ठंडे पानी में) थोड़ा-थोड़ा बार-बार पिएँ। बच्चे को दूध और खाना जारी रखें।

## Diarrhoea: danger signs
Go to a health centre or hospital if there is blood in the stool, high fever, very frequent watery stools, sunken eyes, very dry mouth, extreme thirst, drowsiness, very little or no urine, or if a baby cannot feed.
Diarrhoea lasting more than 2 days in a child, or more than 3 days in an adult, needs a doctor.
दस्त में खून, तेज़ बुखार, बहुत सुस्ती, आँखें धँसना या पेशाब बहुत कम होना ख़तरे के निशान हैं। तुरंत अस्पताल जाएँ।

## Safe water and hygiene
Drink boiled or filtered water, especially in the monsoon when springs and pipelines get contaminated after landslides or heavy rain. Wash hands with soap after toilet and before eating or cooking. Cover food and wash fruits and vegetables.
