# Health in the hills and basic first aid (pahad, first aid)
Keywords: altitude sickness, pahad, mountain, Char Dham, snake bite, saanp, burn, jalna, chulha smoke, angithi, sigri, carbon monoxide, cold, hypothermia, landslide, fracture, wound

## Altitude sickness (high places)
At high altitude, some people get headache, nausea, tiredness, poor sleep and dizziness. Rest, drink water, avoid alcohol, and do not climb higher until the symptoms go away. If symptoms get worse, go down to a lower height.
Go to hospital at once for breathlessness at rest, confusion, loss of balance, bubbling cough or severe headache. Pilgrims and visitors with heart or lung disease should consult a doctor before travel.

## Smoke and heating in winter
Smoke from chulha and wood fires harms the lungs and causes cough, especially in women and children; keep the kitchen ventilated. Never sleep in a closed room with a burning angithi or sigri: carbon monoxide can cause headache, drowsiness and death. Keep a window open.

## Snake bite
Keep the person calm and still, keep the bitten limb still and at heart level, remove rings and tight items, and take the person to hospital immediately or call 108. Anti-snake-venom is available at government hospitals.
Do not cut the wound, suck the venom, tie a tight band, apply herbs or visit a faith healer first. These waste time and cause harm.
साँप के काटने पर घबराएँ नहीं, अंग को हिलाएँ नहीं, तुरंत 108 बुलाएँ या अस्पताल जाएँ। चीरा लगाना, मुँह से ज़हर चूसना, कसकर बाँधना या झाड़-फूँक न करें।

## Burns, cuts and fractures
For a minor burn, hold the area under cool running water for about 20 minutes. Do not apply toothpaste, ghee, ink or ice. Cover with a clean cloth. Go to hospital for large, deep or face and hand burns.
For a bleeding wound, press firmly with a clean cloth and raise the limb. For a suspected fracture, keep the part still and support it, and go to hospital. Get a tetanus injection checked after dirty wounds.
